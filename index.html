📊 How Risk Is Calculated

The 0.00–1.00 risk score combines long-term price trends, weekly RSI, drawdown, relative price position and volatility. Crypto also uses longer-term cycle indicators, with lower scores representing lower historical market risk.

🚦 Signal

The signal converts the live risk score into STRONG BUY 💎, BUY, #HODL, TRIM or SELL. It is a simple visual interpretation of the model’s current risk reading, not financial advice.

🔮 Crystal Ball

The Crystal Ball shows a forward price estimate using analyst consensus where available, otherwise a clearly labelled model projection. The source and latest update are shown alongside the forecast.

🚀 Moon-O-Meter

The Moon-O-Meter lets you move through risk levels from 0.00 to 1.00 and see the modelled price associated with each level. The live price and current risk act as the anchor for the calculation.

🏎️ Lambo Ladder

The Lambo Ladder lists fixed risk levels beside the modelled price for each level. It uses the same risk-to-price relationship as the Moon-O-Meter, giving a quick scrollable view of the full range.
